What a phone holds that was set up with the build before keeping in step (0.3.0), for the
tests of what a build with it does on first opening those files (UpgradeTest, and
scripts/instep-check).

settings.json and playlist-added.json are in the form that build's own code wrote them
(store/SettingsStore.kt and store/Added.kt as of commit 4147858^: every setting by name, the
notes as "lists" and "seen" with no mark), with made-up values. They were written out by
hand from that code, not copied off a phone. PLAYLIST_A, SONG_1 and SONG_2 stand for ids:
the tests put real ones in.

What matters in them: a pin, a theme and two lists' choices, each with a time that build put
on as it was changed, under whichever account was signed in; and a note that dates SONG_2 to
the moment that build happened to look at the playlist (1795000300), which is not when the
song was added.
